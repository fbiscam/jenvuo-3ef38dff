# Chart / signal rules

- Keep reversal pressure scoring deterministic and confluence-gated; a single wick or volume clue must never produce strong confidence, preventing inflated live signals.
- Anchor each swing's pressure to its local closed-candle ATR and body-close displacement so historical percentages stay fixed and later wicks cannot inflate them.
- Expose buyer/seller reversal pressure on every chart timeframe for XAU/USD and BTC/USD but never draw Entry/SL/TP from pressure; the user wants trade plans only from the Zone + FVG strategy.
- Confirm reversal entries only after a closed-candle body break followed by a directional retest-and-hold candle; wick-only breaks remain unconfirmed.
- Require reversal-side buyer/seller pressure above 55% before confirming an entry; neutral or opposing pressure remains an alert only.
- Compute SMC pressure from server-fed closed candles only (live ticks only shape the forming candle) and ignore gold volume; per-browser ticks and feed-specific volume made percentages differ between accounts.
- XAU/USD history: one deep Gold proxy feed (Gate accepted unless clearly broken, last Gate chart kept briefly over a hiccup, thin fallbacks lose to the cached chart, small missing buckets filled with flat bars), spot scale snapped to a stateless 0.0005 ratio grid so every server instance agrees, forming close pinned to spot; provider flips made candles change shape and look scattered.
- Terminal gold history accepts only Gate and the Binance backup, never thin spot-token books or their cached payloads; failed trusted feeds keep the last trusted chart rather than fabricate or clip wicks. Why: Gemini history reproduced repeated flat bodies with extreme wicks in the user's screenshot while Gate's same dates were normal.
- Draw supply/demand zones only on 1m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 45m, 1H, 2H, 4H and 1D (menu switch hidden on other timeframes) from every HH/HL/LH/LL swing: live swings show immediately, confirmed swings need ≥0.5 ATR closed-body displacement, only closed candles break/test a zone, newer zones win overlaps and kept zones keep a 0.3 ATR gap, plain SUPPLY ZONE/DEMAND ZONE labels; the single saved "Zone + FVG strategy" switch (default on, same timeframes; it alone drives zones, their FVGs and Entry/SL/TP — no separate zone/plan switches) shows only the newest 1 zone (once a newer same-type zone has formed, older ones never resurface when it dies) that has its own same-direction departure FVG (drawn with the zone) printed within 5 candles of the swing, and drops it once a closed candle wick hits the SL, and when off shows the classic newest 1 zone + 1 standalone FVG, only zones scoring ≥55 on the deterministic strength score (displacement, outer extreme, wick, freshness, label, departure imbalance) are shown and (classic mode only) a zone is rejected when a newer alive opposing FVG prints within 3 ATR on its side — in strategy mode a formed zone stays until SL wick, close-through or a newer zone, because pullback legs erased valid zones, so only zones likely to hold appear, and zones draw no Entry/SL/TP lines (user request; plan is still computed internally); this keeps zones stable and uncluttered.
- Pin the chart view to the latest candle when history is replaced and ignore empty polls; the index-based view otherwise lands on empty space and candles vanish.
- Liquidity sweeps (`src/lib/chart/liquidity-sweeps.ts`) use closed candles only: untaken 3-bar + 10-bar swings cluster within 0.15 ATR into EQH/EQL pools with a deterministic 0-100 target score (top 3 per side within 8 ATR, best = NEXT SWEEP); a sweep is a wick beyond + close back inside (or next-candle reclaim), a failed hold turns it into a run, and only sweeps ≥40 strength are drawn; this keeps sweep history fixed and uncluttered.
- Fresh FVGs (`src/lib/chart/fresh-fvgs.ts`) use closed candles only: 3-candle gap with a ≥50% body displacement candle and ≥0.1 ATR size, removed on a close beyond the far edge or full fill, newer same-side gap replaces older, only the newest 1 standalone FVG shown when zones are hidden (zone-paired FVGs never hide their own zone); a newer FVG or zone printed on top of any older FVG or zone hides it (resolveZoneOverlaps, render time, always fed both lists regardless of switches), plan moves to the newest surviving zone; keeps gaps identical across browsers.
- Confirmed HH/HL/LH/LL swings and BOS/CHoCH breaks are frozen per asset+timeframe (label and candle never change once confirmed, price follows the candle); only the live, unconfirmed swing may move; a remembered swing re-detected on a neighbouring candle is not drawn twice.

- Candle updates: the chart may update only the last candle in place when every older candle is identical to the previous payload; otherwise it must reload the full history. Why: server-side scale re-anchors or revised candles left stale candles on screen while labels/zones used the new prices, so HH/HL/LH/LL floated off their wicks.
- Refresh cost: chart history refreshes every 15s and pauses in hidden tabs; the live price server poll slows to 15s while the browser stream is live. Why: per-second/5-second server polling drained Cloud credits.
- Gold weekend (Fri 21:00 → Sun 22:00 UTC): the chart drops gold candles that open inside the closed window (the 24/7 feed keeps printing), stops history polling, the live price and the candle countdown (shows 00:00 "Market closed"). Why: extra weekend candles made 1H and other timeframes differ from TradingView; live movement while gold is closed looked wrong.
- "Previous zones & FVG" switch (saved, default off, 15m and higher only) draws the last 10 formed Zone + FVG strategy zones that are no longer the live one, as faded dashed boxes ending where each died (close through, SL wick, or replaced by a newer same-type zone); never carries Entry/SL/TP. Why: user wants to review past setups without cluttering the live plan.
- Gold 45m/2H/4H/1D candles are built from smaller Gate candles with buckets anchored to the 17:00 New York session open (`session-buckets.ts`, used by server and the live forming candle); daily bars are labelled at the trading date. Why: UTC-midnight exchange buckets covered different hours than TradingView, so 4H/1D candles looked different.
- Swing price action (`swing-price-action.ts`, "Swing price action" switch, default on): every HH/HL/LH/LL gets a closed-candle verdict — pin rejection, engulfing, strong reversal (close ≥0.3 ATR past the swing candle within 3 bars), indecision, none, or waiting — plus Buyers/Sellers/Balanced from the swing's pressure (55/45 cut); ✓ only when the pattern and reversal-side control agree. Display only; never creates Entry/SL/TP. Why: user wants to see at each fresh high/low whether price action happened and who is in control.
- Fresh swing sweep (`swing-sweep.ts`, 5M, 15M, 1H, 2H and 4H only, "Fresh swing sweep (5M / 15M / 1H / 2H / 4H)" switch, default on): a swing candle must wick past the previous 10 closed candles' extreme and close back inside (or next candle reclaims), a poke < 0.1× avg range is ignored, a close back beyond the swept level after the reclaim = "Sweep failed (re-accepted)", then a displacement close (body ≥0.3× avg range, ≥0.1× avg range past its body) within 2 bars (a next-candle reclaim counts as bar 1) confirms, candles 3–5 = "Sweep ✓ late move", none by 5 = "Sweep, no move"; confirmed with own-candle rejection wick ≥1.5× body = "Sweep ✓ strong" (gold volume ignored); display only, never Entry/SL/TP. Why: user wants confirmation that a fresh 1H/2H/4H high/low swept liquidity before entering.
- Volume RVOL (`relative-volume.ts`, "Volume (RVOL + Spike)" indicator): drawn in the same fixed bottom strip as plain Volume with no value tags on the price scale; bars keep spike colours by estimated side (bright at ≥1.5×/≥2.5×, amber when balanced) plus a 20-candle MA line; plain Volume is skipped while RVOL is on to avoid double bars. Ratio = volume ÷ average of previous 20 candles (current excluded), ≥1.5× = High, ≥2.5× = Ultra; buy/sell split is estimated from close location (≥60% Buyers, ≤40% Sellers); spike level, Buy %/Sell %, side and "(forming)" show only in the legend; display only, never feeds pressure, zones or signals. Why: user wants spike colours on bars but no coloured numbers, fixed like plain Volume, while gold feed volume has no real aggressor data.
- Terminal Technicals card (`technicals.ts`, tested): RSI/MACD/Momentum + EMA/SMA price-vs-average readings from closed candles of the current timeframe, summarised as Strong sell…Strong buy; display only, never Entry/SL/TP. Why: user wants a TradingView-style technicals view without new trade plans.

- Chart series creation, live updates and script offsets use the shared rendered indicator list; this prevents hidden duplicate Volume slots from shifting RVOL, EMA and script data.
- Gold terminal candles use Gate XAU_USDT gold contract as the primary feed (PAXG token was too thin, candle colours differed from TradingView) (Binance only as fallback): Binance blocks the live server region, so Binance-first made preview and live draw different candles and zones.
- High-probability only (`high-probability.ts`, tested): on every timeframe the chart draws a swing's price-action tag only when it is ✓ (pattern + reversal-side control agree), a fresh-sweep tag only when "confirmed", and pool liquidity sweeps only when held and strength ≥ LIQ_STRONG_SWEEP; this overrides the per-tool timeframe and ≥40 limits above. Why: user wants only the high-probability highs/lows marked, not every reading.
- Strict confirmation: Zone + FVG strategy zones form only from confirmed swings with strength ≥65; drawn price-action tags exclude indecision/balanced control and liquidity sweeps need ≥70. Why: user wants only fully confirmed setups, no fakeouts.
- Sweep timeframes: Fresh swing sweep and Liquidity sweeps draw (and show their switches) only on 1H, 2H, 4H and 1D; fresh sweeps need a confirmed + strong rejection, liquidity sweeps strength ≥75. Why: user wants zero-noise sweeps; lower timeframes produced fakeouts. Supersedes earlier per-timeframe sweep notes.
